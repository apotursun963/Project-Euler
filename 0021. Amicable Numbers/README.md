## Problem
**Find the sum of all the amicable numbers under 10000.**

## What is Amicable Numbers ?
**If 𝑎 and 𝑏 are two distinct numbers and the sum of the proper divisors of 𝑎 is equal to 𝑏 and the sum of the proper divisors of 𝑏 is equal to 𝑎, then they are an amicable numbers.**

## Result
**31626**
